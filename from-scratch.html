All About Electric Guitars

Electric Guitars are Awesome! and they come in all shapes and sizes

here is a list of cool guitar shapes

  1. Stratocaster
    • Most Popular
    • Most iconic
    • Most Versatile

  2. Telecaster
    • Great For Country Music
    • Second Most Popular
    • Known for twangy sound

  3. Jazzmaster
    • Best For Jazz
    • Popular Among indy artists

  4. Mustang
    • Favored shape of Kurt Cobain
    • Rarest of the Fender shapes

  5. Les Paul
    • Best for Classic and Hard Rock
    • Used by Slash and Jimmy Page

  6. ES 335
    • Best used for Blues guitar
    • Used by BB King and Chuck Berry
