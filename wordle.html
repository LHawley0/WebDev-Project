How to Play

Guess the hidden 5-letter word in six tries.

After each guess, the tiles change color:
🟩 Sage — right letter, right spot
🟨 Gold — right letter, wrong spot
⬛ Charcoal — letter not in the word

Type with your keyboard or tap the keys below. Press Enter to submit a guess.
